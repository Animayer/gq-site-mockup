A solar system of subject worlds.

The long-term idea is a hub students can explore. They travel between illustrated worlds, and each world is a subject. History is first. The art is 1930s rubberhose: round forms, thick ink, and motion with a bounce.

Join the waitlist Note for schools

The vision

Kids move through illustrated scenes, meet a short moment from the past, and practice the same skills as tutoring: reading a source, weighing a claim, and explaining a choice. Other subjects become more worlds in the same solar system. Only History is in progress.

History world teaser

History is the first world. A student should be able to walk through a scene, make one choice, and say what changed because of it.

  • A choice with a consequence you can explain.
