About

A studio for history you can retell.

Griffin & Quill LLC is a new edtech business in South Carolina. We help people learn history they can say out loud: what happened, why people disagreed, and which sources support a claim.

Mission

Students should leave a session, a lesson, or a game able to tell the story back: what happened, why people disagreed, and which sources support a claim. We write for parents, homeschoolers, teachers, lifelong learners, and schools.

The work has three parts. Tutoring is online first. Classroom resources will live on Teachers Pay Teachers. Worlds are browser games, with History first and other subjects later.

Values

Accuracy

Names, dates, maps, and quotations get checked before a lesson or a game ships. If a fact is disputed, the material says so.

Fairness to multiple viewpoints

When people in the past disagreed, we show more than one sourced view. We separate what a source says, what a person claimed, and what we think it means. A student can argue a view, and can change their mind when the evidence asks them to.

Accessibility

Clear language, keyboard use, and readable type on this site. Games will need captions and a way to play without a mouse. We are building toward WCAG AA. We are not claiming a finished audit of the games, because the games are not built yet.
