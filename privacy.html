Privacy Policy

Draft placeholder, pending attorney review.

The paragraphs below are plain-language stand-ins so you can see the page. They are not a finished policy and they are not legal advice.

Who we are

Griffin & Quill LLC is a new business in South Carolina. The placeholder contact is hello@griffinandquill.com. This website is a design mockup. It is not the live site.

This marketing site

When a real site replaces this mockup, forms for a consult, a newsletter, a waitlist, a school inquiry, or a message would need a true description of what is collected, why, and how long it is kept. Right now those forms do not send. They do not store what you type.

This mockup does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies. The only third-party request is for the Google Fonts used in the design. A launch version should decide whether those fonts stay or move to files we host.

Children's games and personal data

The kids' games are designed to collect no personal data. A child should be able to play without an account, a name, an email address, or a device identifier collected by us. No profiles. No in-game chat that asks for a name. No ads.

We are building the games this way with COPPA in mind. This draft is not a legal determination. An attorney still needs to review it before any game is offered to children, including children under 13.

Newsletter, waitlist, consult, and school forms on the marketing site are for adults. Please do not type a child's full name or other private details into them.

Tutoring and school inquiries

A live tutoring or school form would collect the adult's name, email, and the note they choose to write. It should not ask for a student's full name in the first message. The final policy has to say who can see those notes and when they are deleted. That language is not written yet.

Teachers Pay Teachers

Classroom resources are planned for Teachers Pay Teachers, which is a separate site with its own privacy policy. Buying there is not a purchase on griffinandquill.com. This mockup does not link to a live store.

Still needs a lawyer

  • Which privacy laws apply, including COPPA and any state rules.
  • Whether a parent consent step is required anywhere outside the games.
  • How long live form messages would be kept.
  • Who, if anyone, would process email on our behalf.
  • What to say if we later add accounts. Accounts are not part of this design.

How to reach us about privacy

Email hello@griffinandquill.com. That address is a placeholder. This page was drafted on October 6, 2026, for the mockup only.
