Tutoring

Is tutoring online?

Yes. Tutoring is online first. You meet by video. If you need a different setup, say so on the consult form. We will tell you if we can do it.

What subjects do you cover?

U.S. History, World History, Government, Economics, and Human Geography. Sessions can also focus on study skills and test prep for those courses.

Who is tutoring for?

Parents, homeschoolers, and students, including adults who want to study history for themselves. If you are a student, use a parent or guardian email on the form.

How does a new student start?

Three steps. A free consult, then a written plan, then sessions. The consult does not lock you into a paid plan.

Do you tutor small groups?

Yes. Small groups sit alongside 1:1 tutoring. A group stays small enough that every student speaks. The example pricing card shows a sample group rate, not a final price.

How much does tutoring cost?

Real rates are not set. The Tutoring page shows example tiers so you can see the layout. Each one is marked as an example. Do not budget from those numbers.

Classroom resources

Where do I buy the classroom resources?

On Teachers Pay Teachers. Buttons on this site are placeholder links. They do not open a store yet.

Can a homeschooler use the resources?

Yes. The resources are for teachers and homeschoolers.

Is there a free sample?

A free sample is planned on the TPT store. The button on the Resources page is a placeholder.

Worlds and games

Can I play a game now?

Not yet. The Worlds page has a teaser and placeholder cards. You can join a waitlist. On this mockup the form does not send.

What is the art style?

1930s rubberhose cartoon. Thick ink, round shapes, and a little bounce. The mascot is not on this site yet. The box says Mascot art coming soon on purpose.

Do the kids' games collect personal data?

The kids' games are designed to collect no personal data. No account, no name, and no tracking inside a game. That is a COPPA-minded choice. The Privacy Policy is still a draft and needs an attorney.

Schools and districts

Can a school license the games?

Site licenses are the plan, and they are not for sale yet. The Schools page has an inquiry form. This mockup does not send it.

Do you offer workshops?

Workshops are an inquiry, not a posted calendar. They can cover reading sources, writing a claim with evidence, and using a Griffin & Quill lesson.
